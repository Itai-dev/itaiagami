Brand Strategy & Identity Systems

An identity is a decision about what the organisation means, expressed in a visual language that still holds when it is used by people you will never meet.

Selected work

Channel 13 A national broadcast identity by Shortcut Playground - I was part of the team from brand strategy through execution. Partner TV One channel-ID system across series, kids and movies, each with its own energy and one identity. Mako 12+ A launch language for a streaming service, built around a single typographic device.

Past projects in this area. My role differed from project to project - each case study states my individual contribution.

When to bring me in

When a new brand is being built, an existing one no longer matches what the company has become, or products and channels have drifted into different languages. Usually a founder, CMO or head of brand owns the decision. I work with them directly, ideally before the deliverables are decided.

The problem

Brand problems arrive as symptoms: no short answer to “what do you do”, campaigns that look like different companies, a product that is better than it looks. Underneath is the same gap: no single idea strong enough to organise everything the company makes.

What I offer

  • Positioning and the central idea, in language precise enough to design against
  • The visual language: logo, colour, typography, image-making and motion
  • The system: how it behaves across channels, formats and other teams’ hands
  • The brand book and kits a designer three years from now can follow
  • Hands-on identity design, with specialists brought in for type, motion, illustration, 3D or the build

Engagement

Full identity systems are scoped once the shape of the problem is clear, with the team they need. The fixed-price Sprint that starts them begins at ₪30,000. If you know your range, say it early: it decides what is possible, not whether the conversation is worth having.
