Fractional Creative Direction & Creative Systems

Many companies have a capable creative team and room for one person to own the idea. With a single standard held in one place, good work becomes consistent work.

Selected work

Natural Intelligence In-house Creative Art Director role: concepts, motion and video for consumer insurance and finance campaigns, tested with growth and data teams, plus an AI-assisted resizing MVP. Kan 11 Interactive Elections A whole broadcast built as one interface, so a viewer learns to read the system once. Kan 11: 10 Facts A repeatable kit that lets an episodic series stay consistent while each episode differs.

Past projects in this area. My role differed from project to project - each case study states my individual contribution.

When to bring me in

For technology companies and scale-ups with designers, motion people and writers in-house but no Creative Director above them: before a full-time hire, while the search runs, or when brand, product and growth each make their own version of the company.

The problem

Without a held standard, teams drift toward the safe version and reviews turn into opinion-gathering. A hundred assets get made and nobody can say which idea was tested. The fix is rarely more people. It is one decision-maker on the creative, and a system that turns production into learning.

What I offer

  • The creative standard: the calls on what ships, with the reasoning made explicit
  • Creative territories, each with its own tone and visual language
  • A working loop from audience tension to concept, variation and learning
  • Regular reviews, and mentoring for designers and art directors
  • Production systems, including AI-assisted steps that remove repetitive adaptation, and hands-on work where it’s needed

Engagement

Usually a recurring monthly commitment (a set number of days for direction, reviews and the work I take on personally), agreed for a defined period and reviewed at the end of it. Retainers begin at ₪30,000. Where a project inside the engagement needs a specialist team, that is scoped separately.
