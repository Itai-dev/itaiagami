How it works

Three steps

1. A 20-minute call

You tell me what’s launching, who it’s for and what’s still undecided. No brief needed. If it’s a fit, I send a short proposal within a few days, usually for a Sprint to start.

2. The Sprint

Two weeks, fixed price. You see directions early and often, not one big reveal at the end. You leave with the idea, how it looks, and a plan for the rest.

3. The launch

If you go ahead, I lead the full launch with the team it needs, or stay on as your Creative Director.

Common questions

Before you write
Do I need a finished brief before we talk?

No. Most people come with a launch and a problem, not a list of deliverables. Working out what the launch has to say, and who we need to make it, is the first thing we do together.

Who is this for?

Marketing and brand leaders (CMOs, heads of brand and marketing, founders) with a launch campaign, a brand refresh or a launch website ahead, where the central idea still has to be found. Past work spans technology products, national broadcast and consumer campaigns, and museums and cultural institutions. You work with me directly, not through an account team.

What does it cost?

A Launch Sprint is a fixed price, starting at ₪30,000 in Israel, $10,000 in the US and Canada, €10,000 in Europe and £8,000 in the UK. Prices are set per market rather than converted. A Full Launch is priced after the Sprint, once we both know what the launch needs: the formats, the team and the production. Creative Director on call is a monthly retainer, priced by the days you need.

Why start with a Sprint?

Because the idea decides everything after it. A fixed-price Sprint lets you see the idea and how it looks before committing to a full production budget, and it gives us both a clear plan and a fair price for the rest.

How do you use AI?

As part of the craft. It lets me explore far more directions in the same time, prototype visuals and motion early, and carry an idea across more formats. More options make choosing harder, and choosing is my job. Where the work needs real hands, I bring in the specialists. At Natural Intelligence I initiated and developed an AI-assisted resizing MVP, so the team could adapt creative across formats without redoing it by hand. See the case → How I direct AI tools →

Can you work alongside our agency or in-house team?

Yes. If you already have an agency or an in-house team, I can lead the idea and direct the work, or work within an established direction.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. I’m based in Tel Aviv and work worldwide, remotely and on site where a project needs it.

Not sure which size fits? Start with a call.
