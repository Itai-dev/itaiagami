How an engagement works

Scope & team
Starting point

Most projects start with a conversation rather than a brief — what you are making, who has to feel something, what is already decided and what is still open. If the problem is not yet defined, defining it precisely is the first piece of work, and it is usually the piece that determines everything after it.

What I take responsibility for

The strategic and creative direction, the central idea, the visual system and the coherence of everything produced against it. I work directly with founders, CMOs, heads of brand, product leaders and curators.

The team

The practice is built around the problem, not a fixed roster. I assemble the senior specialists the engagement requires — strategists, designers, writers, developers, animators, illustrators, 3D artists, film crews and technology partners — and stay accountable for the result.

Budget

Most multidisciplinary engagements fall between ₪80K–₪200K+, depending on scope, specialist team and production. Smaller focused assignments are considered selectively when the strategic or creative challenge is a strong fit. Sharing a range early helps shape the right ambition and team.

Common questions

Before you write
What kinds of projects are the best fit?

Projects where a company or institution is transforming a brand, rebuilding its digital presence, launching something important or creating an experience where brand, content and technology must work as one. The strongest fit is work with a real business moment and several disciplines that need one direction.

What is the typical starting budget?

Most multidisciplinary engagements fall between ₪80K–₪200K+, depending on scope, specialist team and production. Smaller focused assignments are considered selectively when the strategic or creative challenge is a strong fit.

Do you take standalone logo or animation projects?

The practice is built around identity systems, launches and experiences rather than isolated production tasks. Smaller assignments are considered selectively when there is a strong strategic or creative fit.

Can you assemble and lead a larger creative team?

Yes. The practice is senior-led and built around each project. I assemble the required designers, writers, developers, animators, illustrators, 3D artists and production partners, and remain responsible for the central idea, creative direction and final result.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. I am based in Tel Aviv and work worldwide, remotely and on site where a project needs it.

What are you building next?

Tell me the business moment, what has to change and what success needs to look like.
