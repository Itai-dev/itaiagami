How an engagement works

Scope & team
Starting point

Most projects start with a conversation rather than a brief — what you are making, who has to feel something, what is already decided and what is still open. If the problem is not yet defined, defining it precisely is the first piece of work, and it is usually the piece that determines everything after it.

My role on a project

It depends on the brief. Some projects need someone to find the central idea and hold the visual language together; others already have a direction and need concepts, design and 3D that push it further. I work hands-on in both, directly with founders, heads of brand, product leaders and curators - not through an account layer.

The team

I work independently. When a project needs more hands, I collaborate with the specialists it actually requires - designers, developers, animators, illustrators, 3D artists, film crews, technology partners - or join the team you already have. You are not buying a fixed agency roster; the team is built for the problem.

Budget

Independent engagements typically begin at ₪30,000. Larger multidisciplinary productions — where a team is assembled around the work — are scoped separately once the shape of the project is clear. Saying a range early saves everyone a round: it decides what is possible, not whether the conversation is worth having.

Common questions

Before you write
What kinds of projects are the best fit?

Projects where a company or institution is shaping a new brand, launching or repositioning a product, or building an experience people walk into — and where the central idea still has to be found. In practice that has meant technology companies and ambitious products, media and broadcast organisations, museums and cultural institutions, and organisations that need to explain something genuinely complex.

What is the typical starting budget?

Minimums are set per market rather than converted: ₪30,000 in Israel, $10,000 in the US and Canada, €10,000 in Europe and £8,000 in the UK. Larger multidisciplinary projects — where designers, developers, animators, 3D artists or film crews are assembled around the work — are scoped separately with the team the project needs.

Do you take standalone design, motion or 3D projects?

Yes, when there is a good creative fit. Hands-on design, motion and 3D visualization are part of the practice - as a standalone piece, or as a contribution to a direction that already exists.

Can you work with a larger creative team?

Yes. I bring in designers, developers, animators, illustrators, 3D artists and other specialists when a project requires them. When I lead, I keep the central idea coherent across everything the team makes; when I join an established team, I work within its direction.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. I am based in Tel Aviv and work worldwide, remotely and on site where a project needs it.

Have a brand, launch or experience to shape?

Tell me what you are making, what stage it is in and where you need help - direction, hands-on design, or both.
