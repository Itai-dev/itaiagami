Questions

Before you ask

Do I need to come with a brief?

No. Tell me what is launching, who it is for and what is still undecided. If it is a fit, I send a short proposal for a Sprint within a few days.

Why start with a Sprint?

Because the idea decides everything after it. A fixed-price Sprint lets you see the idea and how it looks before committing to a full production budget, and it gives us both a clear plan and a fair price for the rest.

We already have an agency. Where do you fit?

Above it or alongside it. I can own the idea and the creative direction and work with your agency, production company or in-house team to make it, or assemble the team around the work myself.

How do you use AI?

As one tool among many, used to explore directions, prototype motion and interaction and see more of an idea early. The ideas and the final call are mine, and the case studies say where it was part of the work.

Can you handle a large, multi-format launch?

Yes. For a full launch I bring in directors, production companies, animators, 3D artists and developers and direct them, and you keep one point of accountability throughout.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. I am based in Tel Aviv and work worldwide, remotely and on site where a project needs it.

Let’s build something.

Tell me what it is, who it is for and what is still open. A rough idea is enough to start.
