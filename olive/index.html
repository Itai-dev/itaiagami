A slower address

Stone, glass and old olive trees, on a hillside that looks down to the Sea of Galilee.

The place

Between the grove and the water.

The homes sit low among the trees, each with its own pool and its own line of sight to the lake. At night the hillside is a handful of warm windows and the lights of the far shore.

The residences

Built from what is here.

Local limestone, raw concrete and full-height glass. Rooms open to the terrace, and the old trees were kept where they stood.

The house

Stone walls, a concrete roof, a tree at the door.

The rooms

Linen, limestone and the shadow of leaves.

The water

A private pool, held between the trees.

Days at Olive

Quiet rituals, long evenings.
