Old stone, new light

Seven floors of glass and light, rising from a stone house that has stood in Jaffa for a hundred years.

The place

A hidden gem, a lane from the sea.

Bougainvillea over the wall, laundry across the alley, a cat asleep on the sill. East 7 sits inside the old city, not beside it, and the port is a short walk downhill.
