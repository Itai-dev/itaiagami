Privacy

What this site collects, why, and how to change your mind. Written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

Who is responsible

This is the personal portfolio of Itai Agami, an independent creative director based in Tel Aviv. For anything about your data, write to itaiagami@gmail.com.

Analytics, only if you agree

With your permission, the site uses two free analytics tools to understand how people find it and which work they look at:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google): pages viewed, how you arrived (for example a search engine, LinkedIn, ChatGPT or a campaign link), approximate location at country/city level, device and browser type, and a few events such as opening a project, reaching the contact page or sending the form. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies (_ga, _ga_*). Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off.
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft): how pages are used: clicks, scrolling and anonymised session replays and heatmaps. Clarity masks text you type into forms. It uses cookies such as _clck and _clsk.

Neither tool loads until you press Accept analytics. If you press Reject, neither is loaded at all. The purpose is to understand which pages are useful and where enquiries come from, not advertising, and not identifying you personally.

Data collected by these tools is processed by Google and Microsoft under their own terms and may be stored outside your country. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Changing your choice

Your choice is remembered in your browser for up to twelve months. You can change it at any time with (also in the footer of every page). Withdrawing consent stops both tools from loading and removes their cookies from this site; it does not affect what was collected before.

Always-on, cookieless statistics

The site is hosted on Vercel, which also provides basic, cookieless page-view statistics (Vercel Web Analytics) that do not identify individual visitors. To show prices in your currency, the site asks the host for your country code; your IP address is not stored by this site.

Where you came from: referral and campaign information

When you arrive, your browser remembers, for that tab session only, the page you landed on, the website that linked to you (the referrer), any campaign tags in the link (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term) and the list of pages you visit on this site. This stays in your browser and is only sent if you submit the contact form. If you accept analytics, the original source of your visit is also kept in your browser for up to 30 days, so a later enquiry can be credited to it; rejecting analytics deletes it.

The contact form

If you send an enquiry, what you enter (name, email, organisation and the details of your project) is emailed to me through Resend, an email delivery service, together with the referral and campaign information above, and a backup copy may be kept in a private Google Sheet so no enquiry is lost. You receive a short automatic confirmation by email. It is used only to reply to you and to discuss your project, and it is not sold or shared for marketing. Analytics never records what you type.

Booking a call

The contact page shows my Google Calendar booking page inside the site, and the "Book a call" buttons open the same page. It is provided by Google: the calendar loads from Google's servers, and the name, email and any notes you enter when you book go to Google Calendar under Google's privacy policy. I use them only to hold the call.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete information about you, or to object to its use. Email itaiagami@gmail.com and I will respond. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.
