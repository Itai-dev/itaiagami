Claude’s new motion design ability

I gave Claude a short brief: make a showreel, as if it were applying for a motion design job. This is the film it came back with.

The brief

Make a dynamic 15-second motion graphics video that shows what an incredible motion designer you are, like it’s your showreel for a résumé. Go all out.

The result

What’s in it

In fifteen seconds, the reel moves through the basics a motion designer is judged on: a ball finding its timing on a line, type building the word “motion” letter by letter, easing curves compared side by side - linear, ease out, overshoot, spring - particle trails, shapes morphing from circle to triangle, and a dotted planet in orbit. It closes on two words, “Spacing.” and “Feel.”, before the end card.
