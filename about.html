About

Brand / Culture / Technology

I am an independent Creative Director and Art Director based in Tel Aviv. I create brands, campaigns and experiences for technology companies, cultural institutions, media organizations and ambitious products.

My background spans brand identity, broadcast, film, digital products, interactive installations and emerging technology. I work from the central idea outward, defining the story, visual language and system, then directing the disciplines needed to bring it to life.

I work independently and collaborate with designers, developers, animators, illustrators, 3D artists and other specialists when a project requires a larger team.
