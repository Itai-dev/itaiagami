About

I started as a maker. After a Media Design degree from Shenkar, with honors, I spent 2021 to 2023 as an Art Director at Shortcut Playground, making work for Google, MAX, Amdocs, Channel 13 and some of Israel’s leading museums: commercials, a national channel rebrand and exhibitions people walk through. Sometimes I led the team, sometimes I was part of it. Each case study says which.

Then I moved into leading. As Creative Director at Elements, I led the brand narrative, visual identity and product design for a climate-tech platform, and made a complex technology clear across product, web and marketing through launch. As Creative Art Director at Natural Intelligence for 18 months, I developed concepts and campaigns for US insurance and finance brands in competitive consumer categories, and refined them with growth and data teams against performance data. I also initiated and built an AI-assisted resizing MVP that took repetitive format adaptation out of the workflow.

Now I work independently and bring both halves to one launch: the agency instinct for one central idea, and the in-house discipline of making it hold up in every format. AI is part of the craft. It lets me explore more directions early and carry an idea further than one person could before. The drafts got cheap. Deciding didn’t, and that’s the part you hire me for. How I direct AI tools →

I work directly with the people making the decisions and stay hands-on throughout. When the craft needs more hands, I bring in the specialists it needs (designers, developers, animators, illustrators, 3D artists, film crews) and direct them.
