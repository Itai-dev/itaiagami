About

Brand / Culture / Technology

I’m an independent Creative Director / Art Director based in Tel Aviv, working across film, motion, brand storytelling and digital experiences. I develop visual concepts and combine hands-on design and 3D visualization with collaborative creative direction.

My experience spans agency and in-house teams, including Shortcut Playground, Elements and Natural Intelligence. Depending on the project, I lead the creative process or contribute within an established team. AI-assisted workflows are part of how I explore ideas and develop creative work.

My background covers brand identity, broadcast, film, digital products, interactive installations and emerging technology, for technology companies, cultural institutions, media organizations and consumer brands. I’m often most useful early, when the challenge is still unclear and the work is to define what the idea should be.

In independent projects I work directly with the people making the decisions - founders, heads of brand, product leaders, curators - and alongside their internal teams. When a project needs more hands, I collaborate with designers, developers, animators, illustrators, 3D artists and other specialists.

Good fit

Organizational moments

The work matters most at a handful of moments — the ones where a decision about the central idea changes what everything downstream becomes.

If one of these describes where you are, the conversation is usually worth having.

01

Building or rebuilding a brand

A new company, a repositioning, or an identity that no longer matches what the organization has become.

02

Launching something new

A product, platform or category with one window of attention and no story for it yet.

03

Explaining something complex

Technology, science or a service that is genuinely hard to describe — where more assets do not help and a decision about the idea does.

04

Building an experience people enter

An exhibition, installation or spatial experience, where content, space and technology have to be resolved together.

05

A team without creative leadership

Capable designers and no one whose job is the idea — so the standard lives in several heads and the output drifts.

06

Several teams pulling apart

Brand, product and growth each producing their own version of the company, and nothing holding them to one language.
