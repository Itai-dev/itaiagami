About

I’m a Creative Director based in Tel Aviv, working with brands, studios and institutions internationally. My work spans brand identity, broadcast, campaigns, websites and digital products, film and motion, 3D, interactive installations and emerging technology.

I’ve worked in agencies, in-house and independently, including at Shortcut Playground, Elements and Natural Intelligence, for broadcasters, museums and cultural institutions, technology companies and consumer brands. Depending on the project I lead the creative process or contribute within an established team, and I stay hands-on in the design.

AI-assisted workflows are part of how I explore ideas and develop the work. I’m often most useful early, while the challenge is still open and the work is deciding what the idea should be.
