About

Brand / Culture / Technology

I’m Itai, a Creative Builder based in Tel Aviv, working with brands, studios and teams internationally. I move between concept, creative direction, visual design, branding, motion, interaction and technology, and I don’t stop at the idea or at how it looks. I take it as far as the finished thing.

I came up through art direction and creative direction, at Shortcut Playground, Elements and in-house at Natural Intelligence: broadcast identities, national campaigns, museum exhibitions, and launches for technology companies. Depending on the project, I lead the creative or join an established team.

I’m often most useful early, while the idea is still open and someone has to decide what it should be. Then I stay to make it: an identity, a film, an exhibition, an interface, something people can see, use or walk through.

AI is part of how I work now. I use it to explore visual directions, prototype, tell stories and build digital experiences, which lets me take more of an idea from sketch to finished thing on my own. It widens what I can make. The ideas, the taste and the final call are still mine.

With clients, I work directly with the people making the decisions (founders, CMOs, heads of brand, product leaders, curators) and alongside their teams. Studios and agencies bring me in to develop concepts, art-direct or join the team. When a project needs more hands, I bring in designers, developers, animators, illustrators, 3D artists or film crews, and direct them.

Good fit

Organizational moments

The work matters most at a handful of moments: the ones where a decision about the central idea changes what everything downstream becomes.

If one of these describes where you are, the conversation is usually worth having.

01

Building or rebuilding a brand

A new company, a repositioning, or an identity that no longer matches what the organization has become.

02

Launching something new

A product, platform or category with one window of attention and no story for it yet.

03

Explaining something complex

Technology, science or a service that is genuinely hard to describe, where the value comes from a clear decision about the idea.

04

Building an experience people enter

An exhibition, installation or spatial experience, where content, space and technology have to be resolved together.

05

A team without creative leadership

A capable team that would benefit from one person owning the idea and holding a single standard.

06

Several teams pulling apart

Brand, product and growth each producing their own version of the company, and nothing holding them to one language.
