Museum of Natural History

Turning dense science into an experience visitors explore rather than read.

Client
Museum of Natural History
Role
Art Direction, Experience Design
Disciplines
Art Direction / Interaction / Experience Design
Collaborators
Shortcut Playground
The challenge
Create an interactive experience that educates visitors of all ages on the true value of natural environments — without simplifying the science into decoration.
The idea
Working with environmental-science experts, design environments that fit the museum’s world, feel natural, and invite people to interact rather than observe.
The system
Leading a multidisciplinary team of illustrators, designers, animators and developers to build an immersive experience that inspires positive interaction with nature.
The outcome
The experience runs inside the museum, where visitors explore it hands-on.
Role & contribution
I art-directed the experience and led the multidisciplinary team — illustrators, designers, animators and developers — from first concept through the installed exhibit.
