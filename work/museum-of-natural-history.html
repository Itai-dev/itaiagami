Museum of Natural History

Turning dense science into an experience visitors explore rather than read.

My roleI art-directed the experience and led the multidisciplinary team of illustrators, designers, animators and developers from first concept through the installed exhibit.

Client
Steinhardt Museum of Natural History
My role
Art Direction, Experience Design
Disciplines
Art Direction / Interaction / Experience Design
With
Shortcut Playground

The challenge

Create an interactive experience that educates visitors of all ages on the true value of natural environments, without simplifying the science into decoration.

The idea

Make looking the interface. Each topic is entered by looking closer at a scene instead of reading a panel, and the same interface runs in three languages. Developed with environmental-science experts to fit the museum’s world.

The system

Leading a multidisciplinary team of illustrators, designers, animators and developers to build an immersive experience that inspires positive interaction with nature.

What shipped

Installed and running inside the museum: a physical and digital experience built with environmental-science experts and a team of illustrators, designers, animators and developers, explored hands-on by visitors of every age.
