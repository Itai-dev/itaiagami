Simply × on Vision Pro

Showing how it would feel to learn an instrument in augmented reality, before the platform to try it existed.

ASD Tap a key, or play it on your keyboard

The challenge

Simply Piano was launching on Apple Vision Pro, a platform almost nobody had used yet.

The launch had to show how it would feel to play in an augmented-reality environment before an audience had any reference for one, and do it without a headset in the viewer’s hands.

Client
Simply (Simply Piano)
Role
Creative direction and 3D
Disciplines
Creative Direction · 3D · Emerging Technology
My contribution
Led the launch visualization from concept and look development through hands-on 3D tracking, modeling and simulation.

The visualisation of Simply Piano on Apple Vision Pro: the keyboard, the notation and the spatial environments as they would be seen through the headset.

The idea

Design immersive 3D spaces with atmosphere: environments that make learning feel present and physical without pulling focus from the instrument.

The system

3D tracking, modeling and simulation were used to visualize the full experience of playing in augmented reality, from environment to interface.

The room changes.
The keys stay.

The outcome

The visualization fronted Simply Piano’s Apple Vision Pro launch. For most of the audience, it was how the product was seen before they could try it.

Role & contribution. I led the launch visualization from concept and look development through hands-on 3D tracking, modeling and simulation, on a platform with no established visual conventions to borrow from. My scope was the visualization, not the wider product launch or product development.
