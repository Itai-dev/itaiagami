Mako 12+

Positioning 12+ as the home of Israel’s most-loved streaming content.

Client
Channel 12 / Mako
Role
Design
Disciplines
Design / Motion / Broadcast
Collaborators
Shortcut Playground

The challenge

Position 12+ as the best streaming service in Israel: the home of the content viewers already know and love.

The idea

The ’+’ as visual and conceptual anchor, its placement, scale and movement built to spark curiosity.

The system

Design, editing, animation and compositing combined into one cohesive campaign.

The outcome

The campaign launched the 12+ streaming service to the public, on one visual language carried through design, edit, animation and compositing.

Role & contribution

I designed the campaign’s visual language and carried it through edit, animation and compositing to keep the whole coherent.
Film

The 12+ launch film, cut from the content the service carries and held together by the “+” device.
