Channel 13

An identity bold enough to hold together everything a national network broadcasts.

Client
Channel 13
Leadership
Brand Strategy, Identity Design
Project type
National Broadcast Identity
Creative partner
Shortcut Playground
Scope

Logo / Color / Typography / Brand book / On-air channel kit

System

News / Prime-time / Promotion / Continuity / Print

Delivered

A complete national identity system launched live on air

The challenge
Give a national television network one identity strong enough to carry news, prime-time and promotion without breaking character — and legible enough that the teams applying it every day, long after the design work ends, can extend it without asking.
The idea
A bold logo for a bold brand: basic shapes and a confident, pastel-led palette form a flexible visual identity.
The system
A full brand book and channel kit — logo usage, color, typography and on-air functionality — covering every surface a network broadcasts on, so the identity stays consistent across news graphics, promotion and continuity.
The outcome
The identity went on air as a complete national broadcast system — logo, brand book and channel kit governing everything the network shows, every hour it is on air.
Role & contribution
I shaped the brand strategy and designed the identity — logo, color and typography — then built the brand book and channel kit that carry it on air, working with Shortcut Playground on the broadcast application.
Film

A reel of the Channel 13 identity in motion — the mark assembling from its coloured parts, and the colour and typography as they run on air.
