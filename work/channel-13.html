Channel 13

An identity bold enough to hold together everything a national network broadcasts.

Drag to turn itTap to break it apart

The challenge

One identity for news, prime-time and promotion, that never breaks character.

Give a national television network one identity strong enough to carry news, prime-time and promotion without breaking character, and legible enough that the teams applying it every day, long after the design work ends, can extend it without asking.

A reel of the Channel 13 identity in motion, by the Shortcut Playground team - the mark assembling from its colored parts, and the color and typography as they run on air.

The idea

A bold logo for a bold brand.

Basic shapes and a confident, pastel-led palette form a flexible visual identity.

The system

A manual, so the network never has to ask.

A full brand book and channel kit (logo usage, color, typography and on-air functionality) covering every surface a network broadcasts on, so the identity stays consistent across news graphics, promotion and continuity.

  1. Logo usage
  2. Color
  3. Typography
  4. On-air functionality

The outcome

Rolled out as a complete national broadcast system.

Logo, brand book and channel kit covering news graphics, promotion and continuity, built so the network’s own teams could keep applying it long after the design work ended.

Role & contribution. I was part of the Shortcut Playground team throughout the rebrand, contributing from brand strategy through design and execution. The logo, identity, brand book, channel kit and on-air motion shown on this page are the output of that team.
