Channel 13

An identity bold enough to hold together everything a national network broadcasts.

My contributionContributed as part of the Shortcut Playground team throughout the rebrand, from brand strategy through design and execution.

A reel of the Channel 13 identity in motion, by the Shortcut Playground team - the mark assembling from its colored parts, and the color and typography as they run on air.

Client
Channel 13
Role
Brand strategy and broadcast identity
Disciplines
Brand Strategy / Identity / Broadcast
Team
Shortcut Playground (creative agency)

The challenge

Give a national television network one identity strong enough to carry news, prime-time and promotion without breaking character, and legible enough that the teams applying it every day, long after the design work ends, can extend it without asking.

The idea

A bold logo for a bold brand: basic shapes and a confident, pastel-led palette form a flexible visual identity.

The system

A full brand book and channel kit (logo usage, color, typography and on-air functionality) covering every surface a network broadcasts on, so the identity stays consistent across news graphics, promotion and continuity.

The outcome

Rolled out as a complete national broadcast system: logo, brand book and channel kit covering news graphics, promotion and continuity, built so the network’s own teams could keep applying it long after the design work ended.

Role & contribution

I was part of the Shortcut Playground team throughout the rebrand, contributing from brand strategy through design and execution. The logo, identity, brand book, channel kit and on-air motion shown on this page are the output of that team.
