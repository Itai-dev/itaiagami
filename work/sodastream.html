SodaStream

Push for Better: the brand line played straight, with one guest nobody in the room acknowledges.

PPush the lever on the machine

The Push for Better spot: a camel standing in a bright domestic kitchen while nobody in frame reacts to it.

Client
SodaStream
Role
Art Direction
Disciplines
Art Direction · Film

The challenge

Carry the Push for Better platform in a spot short enough to run as pre-roll, without spending its few seconds explaining the product.

The idea

Bring the desert indoors.

A camel stands in a bright domestic kitchen and nobody in frame reacts to it.

The restraint is what makes it land, and the machine stays the only thing actually being demonstrated.

From the dunes to the counter

The system

A warm, daylit set built around the product, with the brand lockup resolving over the scene rather than interrupting it.

The outcome

Produced for the SodaStream Push for Better platform.

Role & contribution

I art-directed the spot: the set, the framing and the restraint that lets the idea land while the product stays the only thing being demonstrated.
