Partner TV

A channel-ID system for Partner TV across series, kids and movies.

My roleI art-directed the channel IDs: the concept, color language and motion direction across all three channels.

The Partner TV channel IDs in sequence: series, kids and movies, each in its own color and energy, resolving to the same wordmark.

Client
Partner
My role
Art Direction
Disciplines
Art Direction / Identity / Broadcast
With
Studio Adam Fineberg

The challenge

Design a channel-ID system for Partner TV spanning series, kids and movies, each with its own energy but one clear identity.

The idea

Vibrant color reflecting the channel’s diverse content, with fluid animation adding movement and excitement.

The system

From concept sketches through palette, animation and visual-effects exploration into a working on-air language.

What shipped

Went on air as one ID system across Partner TV’s three channels (series, kids and movies), each with its own energy inside a single identity, carried from concept sketches through palette, animation and visual effects.
