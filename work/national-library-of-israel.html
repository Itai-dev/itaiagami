National Library of Israel

Making a national archive personal: a country’s history told through the people who lived it.

My roleI designed the visual environment and shaped how archival material, illustration and live footage came together around the narrators.

The narrative film for the National Library of Israel, combining live footage of narrators with archival letters, diaries, photographs and illustration.

Client
National Library of Israel
My role
Design, Experience
Disciplines
Design / Narrative / Experience Design
With
Shortcut Playground

The challenge

Tell the nation’s rich history through the personal stories of its people, drawing on an archive of letters, diaries, photographs and art.

The idea

Combine archival content, illustration and imagery into a clean environment that complements the narrators rather than competing with them.

The system

A multi-format approach combining live footage, graphic design, illustration, sound and animation into one coherent narrative.

What shipped

Produced for the National Library of Israel as one narrative across live footage, archival material, illustration, sound and animation: the nation’s archive told through the voices of the people in it.
