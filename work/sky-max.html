SkyMax

The visual world of MAX’s SkyMax commercial, from concept art to national TV.

My roleI art-directed the commercial’s world and drew the concept art that defined it, then guided its translation into the final 3D elements.

The MAX commercial, with the concept-art world modeled, textured and animated in 3D.

Client
MAX
My role
Art Direction, Concept Art
Disciplines
Art Direction / Concept Art / 3D
With
Roy Kafri, Shortcut Playground, McCann Erickson

The challenge

Position the SkyMax card as a magical card that makes flying abroad possible with every purchase.

The idea

Surreal scenes of puffy vacation elements taking flight with each purchase, developed from sketch into detailed concept art.

The system

Final concept-art elements modeled, textured and animated in 3D for the commercial.

What shipped

Aired nationally for MAX. The world defined in the concept art carried through into the final modeled, textured and animated 3D elements of the commercial.

Credits

Director: Roy Kafri
Creative Agency: Shortcut Playground
Ad Agency: McCann Erickson
