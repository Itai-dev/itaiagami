Taormina Sicily

Projecting Greek mythology onto a Sicilian landmark using 3D scanning and real-time video mapping.

My roleI art-directed the projection and created its 3D content, from the scanned model of the architecture to the environments and marble gods mapped onto it.

A recording of the projection running on the architecture at Taormina, mapped to the building’s exact contours.

Client
Taormina, Sicily
My role
Art Direction, 3D Art
Disciplines
Art Direction / 3D / Video Mapping
With
Yoav Cohen, Shortcut Playground

The challenge

Transform Taormina’s architecture into a platform for immersive storytelling using new technology, without losing the character of the place.

The idea

Drawing on classical Greek aesthetics and mythology, designs were built with precision to fit the exact contour of the architecture.

The system

A 3D-scanned model of Taormina drove the video mapping; generative AI and visual effects created the environments and marble gods projected onto the structure.

What shipped

The projection ran at architectural scale on Taormina itself, mapped to the building’s exact contours.
