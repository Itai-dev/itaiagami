Google Shopping IL

Helping local businesses grow through Google’s ShoppingIL event.

Client
Google
Role
Art Direction, Concept Art
Disciplines
Art Direction / Concept Art / 2D+3D
Collaborators
Shortcut Playground, McCann Tech

The challenge

Show the growth of local businesses through ShoppingIL and encourage Israeli consumers to support them.

The idea

A layered 2D-and-3D storytelling approach that makes the platform feel engaging and memorable.

The system

A set of films built to drive visibility and sales through the event and beyond it.

The outcome

The films ran as part of ShoppingIL, Google’s national shopping event for local businesses.

Role & contribution

I art-directed the films and created the concept art behind their layered 2D-and-3D world.
Film

The ShoppingIL films, in which real local business owners appear inside built 2D-and-3D sets.

Credits

Ad Agency: McCann Tech
