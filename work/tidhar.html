Tidhar

Making the thinking behind a real-estate project visible: architecture with its reasoning drawn back on top.

The Tidhar brand film, moving from the aerial masterplan through tower elevations to annotated interiors.

Client
Tidhar
My role
Art Direction, Design
Disciplines
Art Direction / Motion / Architectural Visualization

The challenge

Most of a developer’s work disappears into the finished building. The planning, the light studies, the green space and the density decisions are all invisible by the time anyone walks through a completed apartment.

The idea

Draw the reasoning back on. Keep the aerial photography and interior CGI real, then overlay the language of the drawing board (dashed setbacks, sun angles, flow arrows, measured spans and highlighted volumes) so the audience sees the decisions and not only the result.

The system

A short film moving between scales: an aerial masterplan with the park read as a single green figure, tower elevations with units and circulation called out, and interiors annotated for light, orientation and materials.

What shipped

Produced as a brand film for Tidhar, moving from the masterplan to tower elevations to annotated interiors in one visual language.

Role & contribution

I art-directed and designed the film: the overlay language of setbacks, sun angles, spans and flow arrows, and how it sits over aerial photography and interior CGI without obscuring either.
