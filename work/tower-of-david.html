Tower of David

Turning the history of Jerusalem into exhibitions you walk through — projection, touch and sound.

Client
Tower of David Museum, Jerusalem
Role
Art Direction, Illustration
Disciplines
Art Direction / Illustration / Interactive Installation
Collaborators
Yoav Cohen, Shortcut Playground
The challenge
Educate visitors of every age and background on the rich history of Jerusalem through interactive exhibitions inside a historic landmark.
The idea
Transform history into an interactive experience — illustration, graphic design and animation working within the museum’s brand and its architecture.
The system
In close collaboration with technology partners, custom applications were developed for projection, touchscreens and sound-guided exhibitions.
The outcome
The exhibitions run at the Tower of David Museum in Jerusalem — across projection, touchscreens and sound-guided spaces.
Role & contribution
I art-directed the exhibitions and created the illustration language, then worked with the technology partners to adapt it for projection, touch and sound.
