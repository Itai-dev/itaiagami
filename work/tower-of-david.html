Tower of David

Turning the history of Jerusalem into exhibitions you walk through, with projection, touch and sound.

Client
Tower of David Museum, Jerusalem
Role
Art Direction, Illustration
Disciplines
Art Direction / Illustration / Interactive Installation
Collaborators
Yoav Cohen, Shortcut Playground

The challenge

Educate visitors of every age and background on the rich history of Jerusalem through interactive exhibitions inside a historic landmark.

The idea

Transform history into an interactive experience: illustration, graphic design and animation working within the museum’s brand and its architecture.

The system

Working with the museum’s curators and technology partners, custom applications were built for projection, touchscreens and sound-guided exhibitions, each one adapted to the room it stands in, inside a protected historic building.

The outcome

The exhibitions are installed and running at the Tower of David Museum in Jerusalem, across projection, touchscreens and sound-guided spaces, in front of visitors of every age and language.

Role & contribution

I art-directed the exhibitions and created the illustration language, then worked with the technology partners to adapt it for projection, touch and sound.
