Now showing

The “Make It Amazing” film, directed by Roy Raz and produced by Rabel Films, set to a track by Noga Erez & RUSSO and released as the centerpiece of the Amdocs rebrand.

The challenge

Give Amdocs’ ‘Make It Amazing’ rebrand a centerpiece: a story about innovation that a technology audience would actually want to watch.

Innovation,
told through play.

The film

Directed by Roy Raz, the film uses a pinball machine as its narrative device: the complex, rewarding path of innovation - navigating challenges, finding solutions, achieving breakthroughs - told through play.

The visual world

Pinball-machine aesthetics meet a precise, nostalgic visual language in a music clip featuring Noga Erez at the center of the campaign.

One machine holds the whole story.

The outcome

The film launched as the centerpiece of the rebrand, with Noga Erez fronting the campaign.

Role & contribution. I contributed to the early concept-development stage, translating the director’s vision into visual concepts for the film. The film was directed by Roy Raz and produced by Rabel Films, with Shortcut Playground as creative agency and McCann as advertising agency.
