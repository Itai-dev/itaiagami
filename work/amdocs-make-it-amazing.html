Amdocs — Make It Amazing

Turning a global tech company’s rebrand into a music-led film people actually wanted to watch.

Client
Amdocs
Role
Visual Concept Development
Disciplines
Visual Concept / Brand / Launch
Collaborators
Roy Raz, Rabel Films, McCann, Noga Erez & RUSSO
The challenge
Give Amdocs’ ‘Make It Amazing’ rebrand a centerpiece — a story about innovation that a technology audience would actually want to watch.
The idea
A pinball machine as narrative device: the complex, rewarding path of innovation — navigating challenges, finding solutions, achieving breakthroughs — told through play.
The system
Blending pinball-machine aesthetics with a precise, nostalgic visual language, the concept scaled into a music clip featuring Noga Erez at the center of the campaign.
The outcome
The film launched as the centerpiece of the rebrand, with Noga Erez fronting the campaign.
Role & contribution
I developed the visual concept — the pinball world at the heart of the film — and defined its look and logic for the director and production team to build from.
Film

The “Make It Amazing” film — the pinball world built around Noga Erez’s track, released as the centrepiece of the Amdocs rebrand.
