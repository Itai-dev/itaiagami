Kan 11 Interactive Elections

Election night as a live interface: results, polls and coalition maths readable at a glance.

Client
Kan 11, Israeli Public Broadcasting Corporation
Role
Art Direction, Design
Disciplines
Art Direction / Data Visualisation / Broadcast Graphics

The challenge

Election night gives a broadcast no second takes. Exit polls, real counts, seat projections and coalition blocs all arrive at once, keep changing for hours, and have to stay legible to a viewer who has just switched the television on.

The idea

Treat the whole broadcast as one interface rather than a run of separate slides. Every screen shares the same geometry, glow and hierarchy, so the audience learns to read the system once and then follows the numbers instead of the graphics.

The system

A touch-driven screen set for the 25th Knesset election: title sequence, exit polls, real results against the counted-ballot percentage, per-party seat counts carrying candidate portraits, regional breakdowns mapped over territory, and live windows to reporters in the field.

The outcome

Produced for Kan 11’s coverage of Israel’s 25th Knesset election, November 2022: one touch-driven screen set spanning title sequence, exit polls, real results, seat counts, regional maps and live field windows.

Role & contribution

I art-directed and designed the screen set: the shared geometry and hierarchy that make every screen part of one interface, the exit-poll and real-result screens, the regional maps, and the live and social windows that open inside them.
Film

A recording of the election-night screen set in use: exit polls, real results, regional breakdowns and live windows moving through one interface.
